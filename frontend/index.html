ENGINEERING INTEGRITY

Why Tatkal Rush?

Designed from the ground up to eliminate race conditions, double bookings, and system crashes under high load.

🛡️

Zero Double Booking

Atomic database row-level locking ensures that only one passenger can hold any seat at a given microsecond.

📊

Queue Management

Partitioned Kafka queues absorb massive 10:00 AM booking surges and serialize requests fairly.

⚡

Fast Response

In-memory Redis token buckets filter abusive traffic, protect database throughput, and speed up lookups.

🔁

Reliable Booking

Unique idempotency keys and automatic seat release workers protect bookings and prevent duplicate charges.
